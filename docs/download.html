Gym Companion

Log your lifts. Watch your lifter level up.

A free gym logger with progress charts, a training calendar and optional friends. No account needed, no ads, no tracking: your workouts stay on your phone and it works offline.

↓ Download for Android (APK) Open the web app
The Android link always gives the newest version. Install it over the old one to update; your data is kept.

iPhone (or any browser)

  1. Open teo2301.github.io/gym-companion in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the Home Screen: it runs full-screen and works offline.

Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above and open the downloaded file.
  2. If asked, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.

Everything it can do

🏋️ Logging & workouts

  • Routines built from your lifts, fully editable
  • Targets for next time (double progression)
  • 190 preset exercises with muscles, plus your own custom exercises
  • Warm-up and working sets, rest timer, per-exercise notes
  • Edit or delete any past workout (title, sets, duration, notes)

📈 Progress & charts

  • One period filter (1M to All) for every chart
  • Tiles with change vs the previous period
  • Training per week, consistency, level over time
  • Sets per muscle, muscle-group trends, training split
  • Workout quality (duration, kg per minute) and best workouts
  • Bodyweight trend; a Table view on every chart

📅 Calendar & back-logging

  • Month grid of gym days back through your whole history
  • Monthly count, sessions per week, current and best streak
  • Add, edit or delete workouts on any past day
  • "Your weeks" card: this week, 4 / 12-week averages, best week

🏆 Strength levels, coaching & PRs

  • Lifter level and tier from bodyweight-relative strength standards
  • Strength by muscle, weakest link and what to train next
  • PR timeline; per exercise: estimated 1RM, heaviest, volume and reps charts
  • Rep maxes, strength-standard lines and "you reach X kg by <date>"

🧍 Character & photos

  • A cartoon lifter that grows with your level, choice of skin tone
  • Optional photo character with tier frame and before/after slider
  • Dated progress-photo timeline

🤝 Friends & sharing (optional)

  • Add friends with a code or invite link
  • Friends-only feed: workout posts, photos, likes, comments
  • Friend profiles: calendar and full Progress, read-only
  • You choose: Share my stats on/off, each photo Public or Private
  • Login link and recovery key to move your account to a new phone

🏃 Cardio

  • Cardio panel with timer, speed, distance and kcal
  • Jog / Run / Sprint intervals, also on past workouts
  • Health-benefits summary after a workout

💾 Backup, restore & offline

  • Works fully offline on iPhone and Android
  • Download a backup (JSON) or your workouts (CSV) any time
  • Restore by replacing or merging; older backups keep working

🔒 Privacy

  • No account, ads, analytics or tracking
  • Without Friends, nothing ever leaves your phone
  • Shared photos are resized and stripped of location data
  • Android app asks for no special permissions
