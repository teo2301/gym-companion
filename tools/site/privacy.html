Privacy policy

Gym Companion · last updated 27 November 2026

The short version

Gym Companion keeps everything on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We never see your data.

What the app stores

  • The workouts you log (exercises, sets, weights, reps, rest times, any heart-rate figures you enter).
  • Your starting lifts, bodyweight, routines, custom exercises and notes.

This is stored only in the app's storage on your phone (or, for the web version, in your browser's storage for this site). It is used only to show your progress, levels and suggested targets.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you choose to export it. Back up and Export CSV create a file that you save wherever you choose (for example Downloads or Files). That file contains your workout data in plain text, so keep it somewhere private.

The Android app does not use cloud backup. When you move to a new Android phone using a direct device-to-device transfer, your data can be copied across.

Permissions

The Android app asks for no special permissions. It keeps the screen on while it is open, and vibrates for feedback. It uses the system file picker only when you restore a backup.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app (Android), or remove the Home Screen app / clear this site's data in your browser settings (iPhone and other browsers). Because nothing is stored anywhere else, that deletes it completely.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/teo2301/gym-companion.
