Privacy policy

Gym Companion · last updated 27 November 2026 (friends & posts added)

The short version

Gym Companion keeps your workouts on your device. There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking. If you never use Friends, nothing ever leaves your phone.

Friends is optional. If you join it, the things you choose to share are stored on the Gym Companion server so your friends can see them (details below).

What the app stores

  • The workouts you log (exercises, sets, weights, reps, rest times, any heart-rate figures you enter).
  • Your starting lifts, bodyweight, routines, custom exercises and notes.

This is stored only in the app's storage on your phone (or, for the web version, in your browser's storage for this site). It is used only to show your progress, levels and suggested targets.

Friends (optional)

Joining Friends creates an account with the name you choose and, if you add one, a profile photo. There is no email address or password: your phone keeps a secret key (it is also in your backup file, so you can restore your account on a new phone).

When you share a post, the server stores what that post contains: the workout summary you chose to include (title, time, sets, volume, top lifts, records, level), your caption, and an optional photo. Likes and comments you add are stored too. Nothing is shared automatically.

Who can see it: only you and the people you are friends with. Someone becomes your friend only when one of you enters the other's friend code. You can remove a friend, or get a new friend code, at any time.

Photos are re-saved by the server when you upload them, which removes hidden data such as GPS location and camera details. They are only shown through temporary private links.

The server is run privately by the app's developer for friends and family; it is backed up nightly and backups are kept for 30 days. Your data is never sold or shared with anyone else.

What leaves your device

Without Friends: nothing, unless you choose to export it. Back up and Export CSV create a file that you save wherever you choose (for example Downloads or Files). That file contains your workout data in plain text, so keep it somewhere private.

The Android app does not use cloud backup. When you move to a new Android phone using a direct device-to-device transfer, your data can be copied across.

Permissions

The Android app asks for no special permissions. It keeps the screen on while it is open, and vibrates for feedback. It uses the system photo picker only when you choose a profile or progress photo, and the file picker only when you restore a backup. It uses the internet only for Friends.

Deleting your data

Friends: Profile → Delete my account and all my posts removes your profile, photos, posts, likes and comments from the server immediately (they disappear from nightly backups within 30 days). You can also delete any single post.

Workouts: uninstall the app (Android), or remove the Home Screen app / clear this site's data in your browser settings (iPhone and other browsers). Workouts are never stored anywhere else, so that deletes them completely. (Delete your Friends account first if you joined.)

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/teo2301/gym-companion.
